Built For Uncompromised Quality & Privacy

🛡️ Source Immutability (INV-01)

Camera originals are opened strictly read-only and fingerprinted with SHA-256 before and after operations. Your raw shots are never modified in place.

🔍 Multi-Signal Candidate Matching

Matches edited images back to source captures using filename edit distance, timestamps, aspect ratios, perceptual visual hashing (dHash), and EXIF remnants.

📱 Full-Featured Native Android App

Complete feature parity on mobile: Metadata Inspector, Format Converter, Continuity Verifier, Candidate Match Review, Batch Studio, and Diff Inspector with zero internet permissions.

🔒 Granular GPS Privacy Engine

Configurable privacy levels: KeepExact, Round coordinates to a 1km fuzzy radius buffer for privacy, or Remove GPS metadata entirely.

💎 WebP & JPEG Modern Format Studio

Batch convert photos with significant storage reduction while maintaining 100% metadata continuity and color profiles.

🌐 100% Offline & Zero Telemetry

Zero cloud dependencies, zero analytics, and zero tracking. Windows and Android applications operate entirely offline.
